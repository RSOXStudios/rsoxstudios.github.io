    RSOX Studios Sketch Card Contest

       

    Each month, RSOX Studios invites artists to submit original artwork featuring Jack the Two Tailed Dragon using our official sketch card template. One winning entry will receive a $5 prepaid card ("Prize"), and all accepted submissions will be featured on our website.

       

    Eligibility

           
    • Artists must be 13 years of age or older, and those between 13 and 17 years of age must have a parent or legal guardian's permission.
      •      
    • Artist must permanently reside in the United States (including all territories), or any country/territory that uses United States currency (USD, $).
